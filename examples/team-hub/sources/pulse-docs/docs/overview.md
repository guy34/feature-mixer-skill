# Pulse overview

Pulse gives team leads a live view of who is working on what.

## Workload dashboard

The dashboard shows, for each person, how many open items they have, how
many items are overdue across the team, and how many approvals are waiting.
Done items are not counted as open.

## Notifications

Pulse can send browser push notifications. See [push](push.md).
Every Monday, Pulse emails a weekly digest of the past week's activity.

## Template gallery

Browse hundreds of board templates shared by the Pulse community.

## Accounts

Sign in with Google. Workspaces have admins and members.
